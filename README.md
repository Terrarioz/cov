# Chronicles of the Void

---

## Overview
Welcome to the cosmic abyss of **Chronicles of the Void**. A thrilling adventure awaits you, where the depths of space intertwine with infinite possibilities. Traverse the stars, meet enigmatic characters, and uncover the mysteries that lie beyond the galaxies. 

## Features
- **Expansive Universe:** Explore various planets, each with unique characteristics and challenges.
- **Rich Storyline:** Choices shape your journey—your decisions matter!
- **Dynamic Combat System:** Engage in thrilling battles with an array of abilities.
- **Class-Based Mechanics:** Choose from distinct classes that influence gameplay.

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Classes
Choose among several classes, each with unique abilities:
- **Warrior:** Robust fighters with high defense and melee skills.
- **Mage:** Masters of arcane arts that manipulate elemental forces.
- **Rogue:** Stealthy and agile, with critical strikes and evasive maneuvers.

### Stats
Each character is defined by crucial stats:
- **Health Points (HP)**: Determines survivability in combat.
- **Mana Points (MP)**: Required for casting spells and abilities.
- **Strength, Agility, Intelligence**: Impact physical damage and spell effectiveness.

### Combat
Strategize in real-time battles. Utilize your class's strengths and exploit enemy weaknesses. Master combos and unleash devastating abilities!

### Leveling
Gain experience points (XP) through battles and quests—level up to enhance your stats and unlock new abilities.

### Inventory/Equipment
Collect loot and equip items that enhance your gameplay:
- **Weapons:** Enhance damage output.
- **Armor:** Increase defense and survivability.
- **Potions:** Restore health or grant temporary boosts.

### Shop
Visit the local shop in each town to buy, sell, and trade items with merchants.

### Story/Choices
Your journey is influenced by the choices you make. Will you help a stranded pilot, or steal from him? Each decision shapes your destiny and the fate of the universe.

### Endings
Experience multiple endings depending on your choices throughout the game. Will you be a hero, or a villain? 

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys:** Navigate menus and select options.
- **Enter:** Confirm selections or interact.
- **Esc:** Open the menu or exit dialogs.
- **ASDF:** Use class abilities during combat.

## How to Run Locally
Simply open `index.html` in your preferred web browser and dive into the void!

## Project Structure Notes
- **index.html**: The main entry point of the game.
- **assets/**: Contains images, sounds, and other resources.
- **css/**: Stylesheets for visual effects.
- **js/**: JavaScript files governing game mechanics and functionality.
